## Install essentials

On Linux and macOS, or WSL on Windows:

```sh
curl -fsSL https://remziduzagac.github.io/skillbook/install-essentials.sh | sh
```

Then ask your agent to **set up skillbook**. [Installing](/skillbook/docs/install/) covers what the line does, how to check the files, and updating, previews and removing.

## The sets

Each set installs on its own, as one skill in your agent's skill list. Essentials is released; the others are still being designed.

- [Essentials](/skillbook/docs/essentials/): how the agent understands, acts, designs and plans, organizes, coordinates and responds, with care.
- [blueprint](/skillbook/docs/blueprint/idea/): the spec-to-dev flow, a project's design record, decisions and tasks, kept in step.
- [softeng](/skillbook/docs/softeng/idea/): how good software is designed, built, tested, reviewed, shipped and maintained.
- [authoring](/skillbook/docs/authoring/idea/): writing for people, such as user docs, business documents, articles and issues.
- [cadence](/skillbook/docs/cadence/idea/): the rhythm of your working days, such as meetings, tasks and reviews.
- [compendium](/skillbook/docs/compendium/idea/): what you learn, captured, distilled and found again.
