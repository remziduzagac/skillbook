# compendium

The first idea of the compendium set. Nothing here is designed yet: every line below is an idea that may change.

## What it is for

A compendium is a collection that sums up what is known about a subject. The compendium set keeps what you learn: it captures notes, keeps the sources they came from, sums them up into short insights, and finds them again when you need them. Essentials makes an agent understand, research and answer well within one piece of work. compendium keeps the results beyond it, so the next piece of work starts from what you already know.

## Its skills

- **capture:** gets a thought, a link or a note down now, to sort later.
- **ingest:** takes in a source: keeps the source itself first, makes a card for it, then pulls out what matters.
- **distill:** turns notes and sources into short insights, each linked back to where it came from.
- **find:** searches what you keep, and writes a short briefing on a topic from it.
- **tidy:** keeps a collection healthy: broken links, notes nothing links to, missing tags and fields.
- **vault:** sets up or reorganises a notes folder, such as an Obsidian vault.
- **teach:** helps you learn a topic over several sessions.

## How it fits

- **Built on essentials:** its research and rightsize rules shape what compendium keeps, so every insight is sourced and short.
- **Apart from a project's design record:** blueprint keeps what a project decided and built, and can be public. compendium keeps sources and insights, which are often private, since a copied source cannot always be published. A project's research notes link to compendium rather than copy it.
- **Beside cadence:** cadence's meetings and close-out hand what is worth keeping to capture and distill.
- **Plain files:** everything compendium keeps is plain text you can read and move.

## Open questions
