## plan

- **Purpose:** planning any work before acting, whether writing, research, design or code: when a plan is needed, how deep it goes, whose approval it waits for, and revising it when the work shows it wrong (D151).
- **Description:**
- **Callers:**
- **Flow** (D151), the direction set; its shape is designed in full later:
  - **When a plan is needed,** judged from signs, not counts: any one of these holds:
    - order matters: several steps, a later one depending on an earlier, or a wrong first step that would have to be undone;
    - there is a real choice of approach: more than one reasonable way, leading to different results;
    - stakes are medium or higher, as conduct's stakes judges them;
    - the work is long or wide: many steps or many parts, where a plan keeps the work from wandering and lets parts go to agents.

    Not needed when the work is short and its answer known, when the person gave the steps, or for a question or an exploration. When unsure, a one-line plan is stated and the work starts.
  - **Depth:** one line, such as "first this, then that, checked by this", when a sign holds weakly; a full plan when stakes are high or the work is long or wide: the goal and how it is known to be done; the steps in order, each with its check; what is out of scope; the risks and what would catch each; what goes to agents.
  - **Approval:** a plan waits for the person when it holds a choice that is theirs, when stakes are high, or when they asked for a plan; otherwise it is shown in a line and the work starts, and the person can still stop it.
  - **Plan mode:** where the harness has one and approval is needed, it is used, since it holds changes until the person approves; where it has none, the plan goes in the response and the agent waits. No skill names a harness's mode; each harness's reference file says how.
  - **Re-planning:** when a step fails, or a fact the plan rests on changes, the plan is revised before going on, and approved again if the first one needed approval.
  - **Challenge:** a full plan is a decision others build on, so it is challenged before it is shown, through understand's challenge (D150).
