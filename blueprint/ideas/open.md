## skillbook as a whole

- **Guidance, not a script** (raised by the person 2026-10-12): settled (D112): main skills hold rules, advice and when to use each skill, with if-then-else only where an order must be imposed, and restrictions only where something real is at risk, each with its reason. Numbers are advice unless a rule cannot apply without them, and advice has a stronger level (D116); conduct was reviewed against it with skillbook-tree (D115) and is reshaped with the flows. The topic as it was raised: skillbook does not give the agent a full course of action; the agent still does the reasoning, and a skill guides it to do things in order. Skills may recommend actions. A wording proposed, not adopted: "A skill guides; the agent reasons. A skill gives the order of the work, what matters at each step, and when a step is done; how to do each step is left to the agent's judgment. It goes into detail only where agents are known to go wrong without it, and says why." To discuss: how it narrows "one full flow, as clear procedures" (D38), and whether act's and respond's designs are checked against it: fixed numbers stated as rules (three failed attempts, the delegation threshold), act's eleven rules, read on every request, and step-level detail such as fanout's findings format; guardrails' detail guards real risks. To revisit with the flows (D108); the checks of conduct still stand. Revisited (D134, D135, D136): conduct's own skill, act, respond, ask, clarify, authoring's markdown and the main skill take the shape.
