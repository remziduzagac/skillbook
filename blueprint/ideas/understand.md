# Ideas: understand

Ideas and open questions for understand, not decided. What is decided is in [../design/understand.md](../design/understand.md).

## understand

Proposed on 2026-10-12, to take up once the sub-skills are designed; not decided:

1. **Its own file holds the discussion and the routing lines,** the discussion as an order to follow: frame what is to be understood and what it serves; restate it so the person can correct it, clarifying when unclear; list the open questions; work through them, facts to research, options to brainstorm, a name to naming, otherwise a view with reasons; close each with a decision or a park; hand back the result. Done when every open question is decided or parked. Since D112 and D118, the discussion would be its instructions in order, each naming its sub-skill: frame, clarify, research or brainstorm, weigh, challenge, then the decision through conduct's ask.
2. **One at a time, or in rounds:** a few independent questions one at a time; many, or ones that depend on each other, in deep clarify's numbered rounds; a recommendation, not a fixed count. How questions are put and their answers taken, one at a time or in rounds, is now conduct's ask (D114); this proposal and the first are weighed against it.
3. **What reaches understand:** a quick opinion stays with act; exploring a topic ("let's discuss X", a decision with several open parts, a plan to question) goes to the discussion; explaining something new ("explain how this module loads plugins") goes to research, which reads the sources and answers top-down with files and lines.
4. **Description:** "Understanding before acting, with the person and from sources: a discussion that frames the topic, restates it, and takes the open questions one at a time, clarifying, brainstorming, naming or researching as each needs." Callers: the main skill; design; test's debugging, for analyze; test's experiments, for research; act, through the main skill, when a request is not understood.
5. **Tests:** a discussion of splitting a repository, closed with decisions and parks; explaining a module, top-down with files and lines; "what do you think about X?", answered by act without a discussion; called from design, the result handed back, not stored.
