# Workstreams

The parts of the work since the initial design was settled on 2026-11-12. Each workstream is a folder here, named by its slug, with its key in the H1 of its `README.md`; its decisions and tasks carry that key. A workstream writes only inside its own folder, and is folded into the root when it is complete. Consolidated, dropped and superseded workstreams move to `archive/`. The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) gives their order.
