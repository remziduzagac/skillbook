# understand

Understanding before acting, with the person and from sources: holds the rules every way of understanding follows, and says which skill to use; it understands nothing itself and never does the work.

## Rules

1. **Understanding keeps nothing:** every result is handed back. Called by a skill, it goes back to that skill. Used directly, it goes to the keeper the project's instruction files point to, if any, which says where it goes and in what form; with none, it stays in the response, and a long result, or one the person wants kept, is offered as a file they name. Each skill here says only what a keeper needs from it and names none of a keeper's steps or files, since a method tied to where results are stored cannot serve every caller.
2. **One result shape:** what was decided and why, what was parked and why, and what comes next; each skill here adds its own part. A caller can then keep any result without knowing which skill made it. The shape says where to find a part and is not a form to fill: a field with nothing to say is left out, except "Not found" when something was looked for, and a field the reader already has, such as the question they asked, is left out of what the person sees. When several skills serve one request, their results are combined into one shape, each one's own additions kept, so the caller always gets one result back.
3. **Guesses and assumptions are marked, and an unknown that would change the result is named with what would settle it,** since an unmarked guess gets relied on as a fact.
4. **Facts first:** read what can be read before asking the person. Asking what the files already say wastes the person's time and hides what only they know.
5. **Who decides:** a choice that would change what the person gets is theirs, put through `respond-ask` with a recommended answer; your own choices are made quietly, with the reason said. This sets where your judgment stops.
6. **Nothing in the work is changed for good while understanding:** reading, asking and searching change nothing; probes and experiments are temporary, made in a scratch place or removed before the result is returned; acting on the result is the caller's, or waits for the person. A change made while understanding decides for the person.

## Advice

- Several skills can serve one request, and nothing limits combining them.
- In proportion: as much understanding as the decision deserves; a rename needs none, a plan more.
- The question itself may be wrong: reframing it is always an option.
- Show what the person would want to correct, such as how you framed the question or what you assumed; your own choices stay quiet.

## Instructions

**With the person:**

- **clarify** (`understand-clarify`): when what the person means is not clear yet, or they ask to be questioned; `act`'s rule to clarify before acting sends here every request not understood; not for what the files already say (rule 4), a topic still to be decided together, or the person not following you (`respond-explain`); it gives back the "going with" line and what was assumed, or a questionnaire for whoever holds the answers.

Anything else about understanding that fits no line here, such as a topic to discuss, a fact to find, options, a choice, an idea to test, a case to make or a name, is your own judgment, under the rules above.

## The last instruction

Before anything is returned: the rules above hold, the result in its shape, guesses marked, unknowns named with what would settle them, nothing in the work changed for good; the used skill's own last instruction holds too.
