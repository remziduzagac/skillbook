# organize-compose

Adds a parent that states what selected items share, keeping their content unchanged.

```text
A B C → P over A, B, C
```

## Instructions

1. **Find what the selected items share.**
2. **Require at least two items and a useful shared statement before adding a parent.** The parent must say something no item says alone. A redundant parent adds navigation without meaning.
3. **Name the parent and write its boundary in one sentence.** Cover everything that belongs and exclude what does not. Follow the `organize` skill's rule for choices shared by its operations.
4. **Prepare the selected items' placement under the parent.** Move them only where folders hold the arrangement and only under the approved map. Preserve their content. Leave unselected items in place. Include every selected item that belongs and no unrelated item.

## The last instruction

Return the proposed or approved parent, what the selected items share and where each item belongs. Check that the parent earns its place and the items keep their content. Keep the `organize` skill's inventory and approval checkpoints.
