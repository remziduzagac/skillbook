# organize-merge

Combines duplicate accounts of the same whole into one survivor, keeping compatible unique details and updating references.

```text
A A' A" → A (the others retired, references moved)
```

## Instructions

1. **Confirm the items are duplicate accounts of the same whole.** Different parts of a whole are not duplicates. A shared part uses `organize-normalize`. A shared theme uses `organize-compose`.
2. **Choose the survivor by the `organize` skill's shared choice rule.** Consider the most complete item, the most linked item or the user's choice.
3. **Prepare the combined content part by part.** Keep repeated content once. Keep each compatible detail that appears in only one item. Combine lists as their union. Ask about contradictions that change meaning through `respond-ask`. End the turn before applying. Do not silently choose a version.
4. **Map every original statement to its place in the survivor.** Include reference changes and retirements in the parent's proposed map.
5. **Apply only under the parent's approved map.** Write the survivor. Keep the other items with retirement notices pointing to it. Update links, references and imports that pointed to them. Keep unknown external references marked unverified.

## The last instruction

Return the proposal and its map, or the authorized result. Include evidence from checking references and accounting for every original statement. Keep every compatible detail. In a completed result, no known reference points to a retired item. Report unresolved contradictions and unknown references honestly. The `organize` skill gets the user's confirmation of the result. Do not claim confirmed completion before the user replies.
