# respond-ask

How the agent asks the person anything, a question, a round of questions or a proposal to approve, and how it takes the answers.

## Rules

1. **A question only when something is truly open** for the person to choose, never by habit, since each question costs the person a turn.
2. **Numbered, each with a recommended answer** and a short reason, worded so that "yes" accepts it; "recommended answer" is the one term for it throughout. A recommended answer is challenged first, its weak points looked for at a depth that matches what is at stake, and its verdict given in one line beside it; a decision the person makes without a recommendation, or against one, is challenged the same way before it is relied on.
3. **Each question under its own heading, its number in the heading,** such as "### 2. How a round is laid out", so the person can answer by number and find each one again.
4. **One round, or one by one on request,** without losing the round: the numbers stay and nothing is dropped; each step names the question by its number and says which remain.
5. **Answers:** "go ahead" accepts the recommended answers for every question left unanswered, and they are said back. When only some are answered, the rest are named back with the answers that will be taken, before anything is acted on.
6. **An answer that changes another question** is said so, and that question is updated.
7. **Parked and assumed:** a question the person sets aside is parked, listed with why; a question still open when the person stops is assumed, taken with its recommended answer and listed as assumed. Both stay listed until answered. A proposal to approve is never assumed: left open, it stays parked and nothing it covers is applied, since an approval must be given.
8. **A proposal to approve** is put the same way, with approval as its recommended answer where the agent recommends it.
9. **A delegated agent** puts its questions in its report, as `act`'s rule on running as a delegated agent says.
10. **The harness's question tool, for a few closed choices:** used when all three hold: at most four questions; each a choice among a few short options; the reasoning fits in the text above it. Otherwise the questions are numbered in text, by rules 2 and 3: when a question needs explaining option by option, a free answer is likely, there are more than four questions, or the person works item by item and accepts with "ok". The person's own instruction file can set a preference, always the tool, always text, or this default, and it wins. In the tool, the other rules hold in its form: the recommended option first and marked, partial answers named back. A delegated agent never uses it, by rule 9. Where the harness has none, text is used. The reasons: the tool saves keystrokes and cannot be misread for a closed choice; text keeps the explanation, "ok" accepting every question at once, and the person's refinements.

## The last instruction

Before a round goes out: every question numbered under its own heading with a recommended answer, or, where rule 10 says, put through the question tool with the recommended option first and marked; any partial answer named back with what will be taken.
