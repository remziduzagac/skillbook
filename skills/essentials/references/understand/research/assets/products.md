# Products as sources

Use this guide for products, competing services and their documented behavior, prices and limits. Compare the applicable plan, platform and date, including hidden costs where evidence establishes them.

Read primary product information and inspect the product where the requested scope and permissions allow it. Distinguish hands-on observations from documentation or reviews. Reviews show their own sample, not every user's experience. Search for competitors' strengths as well as weaknesses.

Use public data unless the user authorized the needed private access. Signups, trials, purchases, outgoing project data or fetched programs follow the `act` skill and `act-guardrails`. If the product cannot be used or current pricing cannot be established, return the unobserved feature or unknown price rather than claiming it was tested.

Independent estimates can help check a market figure when available. Report their origins and differences. Copies of one estimate do not become independent support. The parent and survey own finding strength, comparison terms and coverage.
