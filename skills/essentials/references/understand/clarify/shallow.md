# understand-clarify-shallow

Gets the full picture of a level before going further: one round of questions across all of it, with a recommended answer where there is a basis, then "going with" what was answered.

## Rules

1. **The whole level first:** the level is the request's top level when clarify starts, or a level inside a part when deep reaches one. See every part of it, and leave nothing at it unasked, before any part goes deeper, because which part needs depth shows only once the whole is seen.
2. **One round,** put as `respond-ask` says, so the user answers it at once and sees the level whole.
3. **"Going with: …" before work starts:** say every answer and recommended answer taken, so the user sees what the work rests on.

## Advice

Ask first the questions that others depend on. There is no fixed number of questions.

## Instructions

1. **Look across the whole level,** first from what can be read. Ask only about what you could not fill in from what is known.
2. **Leave out** what clarify's rules say not to ask. If nothing needs asking, ask nothing.
3. **Ask in one round,** each question with its recommended answer and a short reason, as `respond-ask` says.
4. **Wait:** end the turn with the questions. Do not answer them yourself, and do not go on in the same turn with work an answer could change. Take the answers when the user replies, as `respond-ask` says.
5. **Hand over:** a part may have answers that depend on each other and would change the work, or you may be unsure whether they do. That ends shallow for that part. Offer deep on it, as the last instruction says, and ask no deep question before a yes. Deep can take many rounds, and the user may not see why more questions come, or may prefer to go on with your guesses.
   - After a yes, the part goes back to clarify, which loads deep.
   - If deep called shallow, the user has said yes already. Make no offer; the part goes back to deep.

You are done when every question at the level is answered, taken with its recommended answer, parked, or in a questionnaire, or when its part has been offered to deep and the user has said yes or no.

## The last instruction

Before work starts, say "going with: …" for the rest: every answer and recommended answer taken, and what was parked or is in a questionnaire.

In the same response, offer deep for each part handed over:

- Name the part, and say why it needs more questions: its answers depend on each other and would change the work.
- Ask, as `respond-ask` says, whether to question the user on it. Recommend yes, with a short reason. "Go ahead" accepts it.
- If the user declines, take that part's open questions with their recommended answers where there is a basis. The rest become assumptions. Say both in "going with" before any work on the part starts.

Hand it back to clarify. What comes next is the work, or deep on a part after a yes.
