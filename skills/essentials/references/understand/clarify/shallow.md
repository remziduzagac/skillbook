# understand-clarify-shallow

Gets the full picture of a level before going further: one round of questions across all of it, with a recommended answer where there is a basis, then "going with" what was answered.

## Rules

1. **The whole level first:** the level is the request's top level when clarify starts, or a level inside a part when deep reaches one. See every part of it, and leave nothing at it unasked, before any part goes deeper, because which part needs depth shows only once the whole is seen.
2. **One round,** put as `respond-ask` says, so the user answers it at once and sees the level whole.
3. **"Going with: …" before work starts:** say every answer and recommended answer taken, so the user sees what the work rests on.

## Advice

Ask first the questions that others depend on. There is no fixed number of questions.

## Instructions

1. **Look across the whole level,** first from what can be read. Ask only about what you could not fill in from what is known.
2. **Leave out** what clarify's rules say not to ask. If nothing needs asking, ask nothing.
3. **Ask in one round,** each question with its recommended answer and a short reason, as `respond-ask` says.
4. **Wait,** and take the answers as `respond-ask` says.
5. **Hand over:** an answer may open dependent questions that would change the work. That ends shallow for that part. The part goes back to whichever skill called shallow, clarify or deep, and that skill sends it to deep.

You are done when every question at the level is answered, taken with its recommended answer, parked, or in a questionnaire, or when its part has gone to deep.

## The last instruction

Before work starts, say "going with: …" for the rest: every answer and recommended answer taken, and what was parked or is in a questionnaire. Hand it back to clarify. What comes next is the work, or deep on a part.
