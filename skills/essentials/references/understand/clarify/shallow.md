# understand-clarify-shallow

The full picture of a level before going further: one round of questions across all of it, each with a recommended answer, then "going with" what was answered.

## Rules

1. **The whole level first:** the level is the request's top level when clarify starts, or a level inside a part when deep reaches one; every part of it is seen, and nothing at it left unknown, before any part goes deeper, since which part needs depth shows only once the whole is seen.
2. **One round,** put as `respond-ask` says, so the person answers it at once and sees the level whole.
3. **"Going with: …" before work starts:** say every answer and recommended answer taken, so the person sees what the work rests on.

## Advice

Ask the questions others depend on first; there is no fixed number of questions.

## Instructions

1. **Look across the whole level,** from what can be read first. The questions are only for what could not be filled from what is known.
2. **Leave out** what clarify's rules say not to ask; if nothing needs asking, ask nothing.
3. **Ask in one round,** each question with a recommended answer and a short reason.
4. **Wait,** and take the answers as `respond-ask` says.
5. **Hand over:** an answer that opens dependent questions that would change the work ends shallow for that part, and the part goes back to whichever skill called shallow, clarify or deep, which sends it to deep.

Done when every question at the level has an answer or an accepted recommended answer, or its part has gone to deep.

## The last instruction

Before work starts: "going with: …" is said for the rest, every answer and recommended answer taken, and handed back to clarify; what comes next is the work, or deep on a part.
