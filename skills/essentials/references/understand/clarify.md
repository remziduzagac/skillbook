# understand-clarify

Finding out what the person means before acting: decides what to ask and how deep, and sends the work to the part that asks; it asks nothing itself.

## Rules

1. **Ask only what would change the work:** when every answer leads to the same work, decide yourself, as understand's rule on who decides says. An unneeded question costs the person a turn.
2. **Nothing is acted on until the answers are in;** "go ahead" accepts the recommended answers, as `respond-ask` says. This is stricter than understand's rule that nothing is changed for good while understanding, and a variation of it, since it is the person's own wish.

Facts first, who decides, keeping nothing, the result shape and marking guesses are understand's rules; how a question is put, with its recommended answer, is `respond-ask`'s. The result is understand's shape, plus what was assumed when anything was left unconfirmed; each part's own form is in its file.

## Advice

- Shallow first, unless the person asks to be questioned: which part needs depth shows only once the whole level is seen, and one round costs little; many small tasks end there.
- The trigger for deep is dependence, never the number of questions: an answer that opens questions that would change the work. A plan or a design usually has parts that depend on each other, so it usually goes deep after the shallow round.
- When, after the shallow round, it is unclear whether a part needs depth, go deep on it.

## Instructions

- **shallow** (`understand-clarify-shallow`): when a level is not yet seen whole: the start of most clarifying, and each new level inside a part, for deep; not when the person asks to be questioned or for a part whose answers depend on each other (deep), or for questions only someone else can answer (questionnaire); it gives back the "going with" line, or a part handed to deep.
- **deep** (`understand-clarify-deep`): when a part's answers open questions that would change the work, for a plan or a design after the shallow round, or from the start when the person asks to be questioned, such as "question me on this plan"; not for a level not yet seen whole (shallow first), a topic still to be decided together, or questions only someone else can answer (questionnaire); it gives back the summary the person confirmed: decided, parked, accepted and assumed.
- **questionnaire** (`understand-clarify-questionnaire`): when questions remain that only someone other than the person can answer, after the person answers theirs: the person says so, or you see it and ask the person whether someone else holds the answer, rather than deciding alone; not for anything the person can answer, or anything the files say; it gives back the document, in the response by default for the person to send, and later its answers, taken back here.
- **While a questionnaire is out:** its questions are parked until the answers return, and what depends on them waits; the person decides, through `respond-ask`, whether work that does not depend on them goes ahead.

Not here: the person not following you goes to `respond-explain`; "what do you think?" to `act`'s rule that an opinion is not a task; a topic to decide together, or options wanted, is your own judgment, under understand's rules.

## The last instruction

Before anything is acted on: every question that would change the work is answered, taken with its recommended answer by the person's acceptance, parked, or in a questionnaire; "going with" is said or the summary confirmed; understand's rules hold.
